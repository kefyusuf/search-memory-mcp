# P1-04 live provider benchmark — Day 1 of 3 (2026-10-25)

Day-1 measurement of the P1-04 acceptance matrix (≥50 representative queries, three separate days). **This is one of three required days — no routing-profile v2 decision is taken from this file alone.**

## Setup

- Package: local build of `main` at v1.1.0 content; Windows host, TR network, single location/time-of-day.
- Providers: `tavily,exa,duckduckgo`. Official APIs (keys from the maintainer's local client configuration; never committed).
- Query set: `queries-p1-04-50.jsonl` — 50 queries, 36 EN / 14 TR, across technical (13), news (8), research (8), commercial (5), shopping (5), local (5), navigational (5), general (1). The separate 16-query fixture (`queries.jsonl`) keeps broader general coverage; a future revision may rebalance general.
- Command: `node scripts/benchmark-providers.mjs evals/providers/queries-p1-04-50.jsonl --json 2026-10-25-p104-day1-attempts.json` with 1500 ms inter-call delay.

## Reading (preliminary, day 1 of 3)

- **No blocking anywhere**, including the scraper baseline: DuckDuckGo answered all 50 — consistent with the October 2026 finding that it is the only scraper answering consistently, and much better than the same month's Brave/Google/Yahoo/Bing scraper results.
- **Tavily and Exa official APIs are reliable and predictable** from this network; both sit in the 1.8–2.1 s p50 band, Exa with a longer tail (p95 3.7 s).
- DuckDuckGo remains the fastest path by a wide margin; the API providers buy capability (news topic + publish dates; neural retrieval), not speed.
- Days 2–3 must sample different times of day before any availability claim; this run says nothing about quota exhaustion over a month or rate-limit behavior under concurrency.

## Interpretation limits

- Single day/location; success-rate comparisons across providers need the full 3-day matrix.
- The benchmark measures provider reachability and parseability, not result quality (P1-03's offline set addresses ranking quality).
- Raw attempts: `2026-10-25-p104-day1-attempts.json` in this directory.
